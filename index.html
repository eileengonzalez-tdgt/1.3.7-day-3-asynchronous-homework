01 · Speaking · 15 min

Explain Your Role & Give Directions

Record a 2–3 minute message for a new coworker who is visiting your workplace for the first time. Include:

  1. Two responsibilities using I'm in charge of / I've been assigned to / I handle.
  2. One issue you might look into and how you will keep someone updated.
  3. Clear directions to the service desk using first, next, finally and imperatives.
  4. One polite request for confirmation.

Record using Vocaroo. Share your recording according to your trainer's instructions.

03 · Reading · 10 min

Reading: A Change of Plans at Northgate

Read the internal operations update carefully. Identify responsibilities, deadlines, reasons for changes, and what staff should do next. Then answer 10 comprehension questions.

Northgate Training Center | Operations Update

To: New hires and orientation support team
From: Maya Chen, Operations Coordinator
Subject: Updated orientation arrangements for July 14

Thank you for confirming your attendance at next Tuesday’s orientation. The session will still take place on July 14, from 9:00 to 11:00 AM, but we have made several changes to the arrival procedure. The original check-in area at the main entrance is temporarily unavailable because maintenance staff are replacing the security system. Instead, please report to the service desk on the ground floor of the east building, at 24 Oak Street. The east entrance is beside the public library, not the entrance facing the parking lot.

Although orientation begins at 9:00 AM, everyone must complete identity verification by 8:45 AM. Please bring a photo ID and the confirmation email you received last week. Do not share your account password with anyone, including the check-in team. If your confirmation email has not arrived by Friday, contact us before the weekend so we can follow up on the request.

Responsibilities have also been reassigned. Jordan is in charge of setting up the temporary welcome area and making sure the signs are visible from Oak Street. Alex has been assigned to coordinate with the building team and direct visitors from the library to the correct entrance. Maya will handle identity-verification questions and look into any registration problems. Because the support team may be busy during the first 15 minutes, please avoid asking several employees to investigate the same issue. One team member will take responsibility and keep you updated.

Finally, participants joining the optional afternoon workshop should note that it runs from 2:00 to 4:00 PM in Room 204, not the training room used in the morning. The workshop is open to new hires who have completed orientation; attendance is not required. If you are unsure which session you registered for, please ask: “Could you confirm whether I’m registered for the afternoon workshop?” We appreciate your flexibility while the building improvements are completed.

04 · Listening & Pronunciation · 15 min

Listening: A Last-Minute Orientation Change

Audio provided by your trainer

Your trainer will provide a recording of a realistic workplace call. Listen once for the main issue, then a second time for responsibilities, timing, and the agreed next steps. Answer the 10 questions before revealing the transcript.

Reveal transcript after listening

Alex: Hi, Jordan. I’m on Oak Street, just outside the library. I followed the address in yesterday’s email, but the main entrance is closed. Could you clarify where I’m supposed to check in?

Jordan: Of course, and sorry for the confusion. We ran into an issue with the security system, so check-in has been moved to the east building. Are you facing the library entrance?

Alex: Yes. There’s a parking lot on my right and a small café across the street.

Jordan: Perfect. Walk north along Oak Street, past the café. At the next intersection, turn right, then look for a blue sign that says “Visitor Check-In.” Don’t use the door next to the parking lot; it leads to the maintenance area.

Alex: Let me repeat that to make sure I understood: go north, pass the café, turn right at the intersection, and follow the blue sign. Is that correct?

Jordan: Exactly. And make sure you have your photo ID and confirmation email ready. The session starts at nine, but verification must be completed by eight forty-five.

Alex: I have my ID, but I can’t find the email. I thought we could bring it later.

Jordan: The email is required for check-in, but we can help you retrieve it. I’m in charge of the welcome area, so I can’t leave my station. Maya handles registration problems. I’ll coordinate with her and ask her to meet you at the service desk.

Alex: Would it be possible to confirm whether I’m also registered for the afternoon workshop? I remember signing up, but I’m not completely sure.

Jordan: Maya can verify that as well. Just so you know, the workshop is optional and runs from two to four in Room 204. It isn’t in the same room as the morning orientation.

Alex: Thanks. Should I wait outside until Maya arrives?

Jordan: No, please go straight to the service desk. I’ll follow up with Maya now, and I’ll keep you updated if anything changes. Were you able to find the blue sign?

Alex: Yes, I can see it. Thanks for helping me sort this out.

Shadowing

Listen again and shadow these five phrases. Focus on connected speech, polite intonation, and stress on key information:

  1. Could you clarify where I’m supposed to check in?
  2. Let me repeat that to make sure I understood.
  3. Make sure you have your photo ID and confirmation email ready.
  4. Would it be possible to confirm whether I’m also registered?
  5. I’ll follow up with Maya now, and I’ll keep you updated.
05 · Writing · 5 min

Help a Customer Find Their Way

A customer says: “I have an appointment on Friday between 9:00 and 11:00 AM. I think it's at Oak Street in Toronto, but I don't have the building number. Can you help?”

Write a 40–70 word professional response. Ask for the missing address information, verify the appointment time, and explain what you will do next. Include at least one polite request and one workplace expression such as look into, follow up on, make sure.

This check reviews length and language features, not whether your response is fully accurate. Your trainer reviews the writing.
